Car step: three directions

Each direction shows the saved-car picker and the add-a-car form, in light and dark mode, using IRIS's real violet, Inter and theme colors. Sample cars are made up. Pick one (or mix parts) and I'll build it.

A. Plate list

Each saved car is a soft row you tap, with its licence plate drawn as a real plate. Filters show only when you own more than one type. "Add a new car" is the last row. The form puts car type in one row at the top, uses color swatches instead of typing, and previews the plate as you type.

C. Sorted by size
