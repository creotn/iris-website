Profile

Balance Card

Full blue gradient #0B96D0→#00ACE4 with decorative circles. Growth badge uses rgba(0,0,0,.2) overlay for contrast on the gradient.

Bar Chart

Pure CSS bar chart. Bars use #2C2C2E default, peak day highlighted with blue gradient. Heights are percentage-based for responsive scaling.

Payout Rows

Outgoing amounts shown in #DC3545 red with prefix dash. Status "✓ Sent" in #00C87A green. Bank icon uses #2C2C2E pill container.

Period Toggle

Three-chip selector: This Week / This Month / All Time. Selected chip uses gradient fill. Data refreshes based on period selection via API.

Request Payout

Bottom CTA triggers withdrawal flow. Minimum payout threshold enforced. Real-time available balance shown separately from pending.

Commission Structure

Platform takes 25% commission. Net earnings after commission are displayed everywhere — the professional always sees what they actually keep, never the gross service price.

Job Count & Averages

Balance card shows 47 Jobs and Avg 7.29 DT/job as meta chips on the gradient. These give at-a-glance performance KPIs without navigating away.

Growth Indicator

Month-over-month comparison shown as a percentage badge. Green when positive, red when below previous month. Helps professionals track business momentum.
