4-Step Progress Tracker

Horizontal stepper uses #00C87A for completed steps, #0B96D0 for the active step, and muted gray for upcoming. Connector lines mirror the step state.

Live Timer

Once the professional marks "Arrived" and starts the job, a timer begins counting up in #0B96D0 blue. Monospaced digits use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.

Wash Checklist

Interactive checklist lets the professional track each wash phase. Checked items turn #00C87A green. Helps ensure quality control and serves as a completion confirmation.

Action Chips

Call and Message chips on the customer card use #2C2C2E background. Tapping "Call" triggers a native phone call. "Message" opens an in-app chat thread.
