Job Card Components

Each card shows service type, price, customer rating, distance, tool availability, and net earnings. Cards use #1C1C1E background with border-radius: 14px. Taken jobs are shown at 55% opacity.

Earnings Formula

Net earnings displayed in #00C87A green. Platform commission is 25% — deducted visually on the detail screen before the professional accepts. Full breakdown shown pre-commit.

Online Status Toggle

Green badge rgba(0,200,122,.12) with pulsing dot. Toggle controls job visibility in the feed. Going offline hides the professional from the dispatch queue entirely.

Map Detail View

Dark map style #0D1A24 → #1A2A3A gradient. Shows estimated drive distance and time before accepting. Customer pin uses IRIS blue #0B96D0 with glow shadow.
